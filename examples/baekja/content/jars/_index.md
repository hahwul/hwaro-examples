Six jars, fired between the thaw of 2025 and the first heavy snow of 2026. They stand below on one ground line and at one scale, so that the small one looks small. Each is named for the weather on the day the kiln was lit. The register beneath them gives what I measured after the fire and the one fault in each that I decided not to mind.
